## Research Boundary

- No blog, Reddit post, legal marketing page, Yuka page or Open Food Facts page was treated as legal authority.
- Source statements were used to identify engineering risk and manual review steps, not to answer unresolved Turkish-law questions.
- The live legal pages linked by the app were separately fetched on 8 August 2026 for product audit; they are not official legal sources.
