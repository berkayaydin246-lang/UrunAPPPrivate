# Product Score Dispute Playbook

Status: internal operational draft. Legal demands must be handled by licensed
counsel; this is not a confrontation or admission template.

## Scenario

A company challenges a product score or demands removal, potentially with legal
language.

## Internal Process

1. Do not argue publicly or speculate about the company, product or law.
2. Preserve the currently displayed data, score, methodology/code version,
   sources, screenshots and timestamps.
3. Identify the exact product, market variant, barcode and disputed score/data
   version.
4. Reproduce the score from the stored current inputs and record all components,
   readiness and versions.
5. Verify the source label/data and whether the physical product changed.
6. Inspect the reporter's evidence for product/variant/date/authority match.
7. Correct factual product data through normal review if credible evidence proves
   it wrong.
8. Recalculate automatically with the active deterministic methodology.
9. Never manually alter formula, threshold, classification or score for the
   company.
10. Escalate legal demands, trademark issues, injunction/takedown demands and
    admissions to counsel.
11. Preserve correspondence and decision records under the approved retention/
    legal-hold process.
12. Avoid admissions, guarantees, blame and unsupported legal/scientific claims.

## Notice/Takedown Principle

- A factual score is not removed solely because a company dislikes it.
- Credible factual error receives prompt review/correction.
- Legal demand triggers evidence preservation and counsel escalation.
- Manifestly wrong/unreliable data may require temporarily marking the score
  unavailable while verification occurs.
- No automatic takedown behaviour is implemented in this phase.

## Evidence Package

- product/barcode/market variant;
- product/scoring input snapshot and source material;
- score/readiness/component/methodology versions;
- canonical additive assessment and risk-catalogue version, when available;
- public UI wording and screenshots;
- correction/report records and correspondence;
- commercial relationship, if any.
