# Etiketly Gizlilik Politikası v2 - Değişiklik Seti

> **YAYIN DURUMU: LOCAL DRAFT ONLY. CANLI GİZLİLİK POLİTİKASI GÜNCELLENMEDİ.**
>
> **OPERATOR IDENTITY REQUIRED BEFORE PUBLICATION**

Current live page audited: `privacy.html`, last-updated label **30 June 2026**.
It describes camera/photos, OCR, product reports, app-install ID, Supabase,
Render, Anthropic and Google Play. The following changes are required before it
can represent the current score/report/submission release accurately.

## Required Additions/Corrections

### 1. Controller Identity

Add the controller's full legal identity, address/contact, representative if
applicable and KVKK application channel. A personal support email alone does not
satisfy the missing identity fields.

### 2. Activity-Specific Processing

Describe actual activities separately:

- consumer use of public product catalogue and network requests;
- local favourites and recently viewed/scanned history (currently device-local
  SharedPreferences unless another sync feature is introduced);
- product submissions: product name/barcode, notes, front/ingredient/nutrition
  photos, OCR/extracted data and admin status;
- product reports: product snapshot, report type, optional text, random install
  and submission IDs, status/admin review;
- OCR: camera/gallery image, extracted text, temporary backend processing and
  actual providers;
- admin authentication: admin email/account/session via Supabase Auth;
- manufacturer corrections/support correspondence, if launched;
- operational logs actually generated by backend/providers.

Do not claim analytics, advertising IDs, crash reporting, location, contacts or
health-profile processing unless those features are actually introduced. No
Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, PostHog or ad SDK was found in the
current Flutter dependencies.

### 3. Purpose And Legal Basis

For each activity state a specific purpose and exact KVKK Articles 5/6 condition.
Avoid “may process for any future improvement”. **COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED** for
legal bases; do not use consent as a generic fallback.

### 4. Recipients And International Transfer

Name recipient groups/purposes and reflect actual architecture: Supabase,
Render, Anthropic where advanced OCR is used, Google/Play, hosting/support and
authorised admins. Verify provider entities, regions and contracts.

**COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED:** Determine and document the applicable KVKK overseas
transfer mechanism. The policy must not say data stays in Turkey unless verified.

### 5. Retention And Deletion

Replace “reasonable periods” and “best effort” as the only controls with a
retention schedule and implemented deletion process for:

- temporary OCR images and backend logs;
- pending/approved/rejected submission images;
- product reports and random install IDs;
- admin auth/security logs;
- correction/dispute evidence and correspondence;
- local favourites/history and app deletion/clear actions.

Do not promise immediate deletion if provider logs/backups make that untrue.
State criteria and backup effects accurately after engineering verification.

### 6. Rights And Requests

List Article 11 rights and explain how identity is proportionately verified,
how requests are submitted and handled, and how deletion may interact with
necessary dispute/security records. Do not collect excess identity evidence.

### 7. Score And Health Boundary

Clarify that Etiketly Puanı is computed from product data, not the user's health
profile. The current app does not use user health data to personalize the score.
Do not state “we never process health data” while free-text/photo fields could
receive unintended sensitive content; instead instruct users not to submit it
and define handling/deletion.

## Article 10 Collection-Point Changes

A generic privacy page is not by itself a substitute for activity-specific
notice. Before report submission, product/photo submission and future
manufacturer contact, show a concise layered notice linking to the relevant full
section and identifying controller, purpose, legal basis, recipient/transfer and
rights.

Official basis: [KVKK July 2026 clarification](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6765/AYDINLATMA-YUKUMLULUGUNUN-YERINE-GETIRILMESI-HAKKINDA-KAMUOYU-DUYURUSU).
If that URL changes, use the source-register link before publication.

## Publication Gate

Do not label this changeset “effective” until controller identity, legal bases,
transfers, retention and actual provider facts are approved and the public HTML
is updated. App link currently remains unchanged.
