# Public Claims And Commercial Influence Policy

Status: internal publication rule for product, marketing and future commercial
teams. **COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED** before monetized ranking/recommendation launch.

## Product-Focused Language

- Evaluate the product data snapshot and methodology result, not a company's
  morality, honesty, intent or overall conduct.
- State the product, barcode, data date and methodology version where a dispute
  or formal comparison requires precision.
- Do not call a manufacturer “bad”, “deceptive”, “irresponsible” or similar
  based solely on a product score.
- A data correction changes the verified input and triggers deterministic
  recalculation; it never permits negotiated formula changes.

## Prohibited Public Concepts

Do not use an Etiketly score to claim:

- healthy/unhealthy product;
- safe/dangerous product;
- toxic, carcinogenic or disease-causing product;
- approved/rejected by a regulator;
- recommended by a doctor, authority, manufacturer or Etiketly for an
  individual;
- best, healthiest or safest product;
- guaranteed complete, current or error-free data.

Claim-specific scientific statements require a traceable primary source,
editorial verification, date/version and counsel decision; a generic disclaimer
does not make an unsupported claim acceptable.

## Safer Public Concepts

- “İçerik profili: İyi.”
- “Etiketly katkı değerlendirmesi: yüksek düzey.”
- “Etikette tespit edilen alerjenler.”
- “Bu ölçütte değer daha düşük/yüksek.”
- “Etiketly Puanı daha yüksek alternatif” only when score readiness, version and
  comparison policy requirements are all met.

## Future Advertising And Sponsorship

If commercial content is introduced:

- label it prominently as `Reklam`, `Sponsorlu içerik`, `İş birliği` or another
  counsel-approved clear label;
- never allow payment or commercial benefit to alter the formula, verified
  input, score, explanation or correction outcome;
- never disguise sponsored placement as organic ranking;
- never suppress a lower score or factual correction because of sponsorship;
- disclose any ranking effect before launch; preferred policy is no effect on
  organic ranking;
- retain campaign evidence and approval records under a counsel-approved
  retention schedule.

The 1 August 2026 digital advertising changes are recorded in the
[official source register](./OFFICIAL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md). No advertising or
sponsored-ranking code is implemented in this phase.
