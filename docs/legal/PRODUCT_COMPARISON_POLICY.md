# Product Comparison And Alternative Policy

Status: publication/engineering rule. Application to Turkish comparative
advertising and unfair competition is **COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED**, particularly
before monetisation.

## Eligible Comparison

- Compare related products that meet the same need or purpose.
- Use objective, current, sufficiently verified data.
- Compare only compatible bases (for example 100 g with 100 g, not 100 g with a
  serving); current comparison code blocks unknown/incompatible bases.
- Identify missing data rather than treating it as zero or inferior.
- Preserve data source/date and methodology version for formal claims.

## Public Wording

Allowed when factually supported:

- “Şeker değeri daha düşük.”
- “Lif değeri daha yüksek.”
- “Bu ölçütte vurgulanan değer.”
- “Etiketly Puanı daha yüksek alternatif” only when both scores are ready,
  calculated under the same methodology version and the products are related.

Do not say:

- healthier/healthiest;
- safer/safest;
- best product;
- medically suitable;
- the manufacturer is bad, deceptive or irresponsible;
- a lower score proves illegality or product danger.

## Score And Explanation

Display the actual data and methodology basis. Score explanation must remain
factual and non-personalised. It must not tell a specific user what they should
eat based on a medical/dietary condition. Allergen comparison must say
“etikette tespit edilen” and direct users to the current package.

## Commercial Separation

- Sponsored content must be clearly labelled.
- Payment cannot change score, verified input, formula or correction outcome.
- Sponsorship must not be disguised as organic comparison/ranking.
- Do not suppress lower scores or factual errors for a partner.
- Any future paid ranking effect requires explicit disclosure, engineering
  separation and counsel approval before launch.

## Record Required For A Published Comparative Claim

- product IDs/barcodes and market variant;
- product data snapshots and source dates;
- compatible measurement basis;
- score/methodology version where used;
- generated comparison result and publication date;
- commercial relationship/disclosure, if any.

Official background is listed in
[OFFICIAL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md](./OFFICIAL_SOURCE_REGISTER.md), including Ministry
of Trade comparative-advertising guidance and Turkish Commercial Code Articles
54-55. This policy does not conclude that every editorial comparison is or is
not a commercial advertisement.
